Zorva — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Naeem Anjum · Contact: naeemanjum076@gmail.com

What Zorva does

Zorva is a Windows app that turns long videos into short clips. You paste a video link (for example a YouTube link) or choose a video file on your PC; Zorva downloads the video, transcribes it, uses a local AI model to suggest the strongest moments, and exports polished clips with captions. All of this processing happens entirely on your own PC — the transcription and AI models are bundled with the app and run locally. Zorva has no cloud component of any kind.

Data we collect

None. Zorva has no user accounts, we run no servers for it, and the app contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Information stored on your PC

Videos you download through Zorva, their transcripts, the clips and caption styles you create, your project library, and your settings are stored locally in Zorva's data folder on your PC. Exported clips are written to the export folder you choose. None of this information ever leaves your device. You can delete projects and models from within the app at any time (deleting a project never deletes clips you already exported), and uninstalling Zorva removes the app; its local data folder can be deleted like any other folder.

When Zorva uses the internet

Zorva connects to the internet only for the following, and only at your direction:

Video downloads. When you paste a link and start a job, Zorva downloads that video directly from the platform you linked (for example YouTube) to your PC, using the open-source yt-dlp tool. The request goes from your PC to that platform under the platform's own privacy policy; nothing passes through us.

Model downloads. Zorva's AI models are bundled with the app. If you choose to download an additional or larger model in the Models tab, it is downloaded from Hugging Face directly to your PC and verified against a published checksum.

App updates. Zorva checks GitHub Releases for new versions and downloads updates from GitHub when available. As with any download, GitHub can see your IP address and which file was requested, under its own privacy policy.

Zorva has no other network use: it uploads nothing, and transcription, moment-finding, and exporting all work fully offline.

Your content

You are responsible for the videos you download and clip with Zorva. Please only download content you own or have permission to use, and respect the terms of the platform it came from.

Children

Zorva is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
